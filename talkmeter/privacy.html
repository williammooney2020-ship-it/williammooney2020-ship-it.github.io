TalkMeter Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

In short: TalkMeter analyses sound on your iPhone and keeps only counts. We never record, store, transcribe or upload audio, and we don't collect any personal data on servers. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Microphone and audio

TalkMeter uses the microphone only while a listening session you started is running, or while you record a voice sample during setup. Audio is processed in your iPhone's memory in real time, using on-device processing including Apple's built-in sound recognition, and is discarded within seconds. No audio, words or transcripts are ever saved to disk or sent anywhere. While a session runs, iOS shows the microphone indicator and TalkMeter shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen.

What is stored on your device

  • Your child's first name, birthday and daily goal, which you enter.
  • Session results: start and end time, minutes listened, estimated grown-up words, child sounds and back-and-forth turns.
  • Voice fingerprints: a handful of numbers describing the pitch and brightness of each voice you calibrate. These cannot be turned back into audio.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone and is included in your device backups, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see or access it.

What we collect

Nothing. TalkMeter has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple shares only anonymous sales reports with developers.

Children

TalkMeter is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. Sound from children near the phone is analysed only in memory as described above and is never stored or shared. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase all TalkMeter data in the app, or delete the app. Because nothing is held on our side, there is nothing else to delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date. Questions: contact us.
